# 🌾 AgriTech Business & Data Analyst Workspace

This directory contains all **Business & Data Analysis** deliverables, requirements documentation, and economic impact models across the 8-week timeline for the **AgriTech Decision-Support & AI Platform** project.

---

## 👤 Role Overview
- **Title:** Business & Data Analyst - AgriTech
- **Core Focus:** Bridging agricultural field operations with AI technology, framing business requirements (BRD), defining economic impact (ROI & KPI models), validating agricultural accuracy, and designing business management dashboards.

---

## 📂 Owned Deliverables
1. **Business Requirements Document (BRD)**
2. **Economic Impact & ROI Models**
3. **Analytical Management Dashboards (Specs & Mockups)**
4. **End-User Manual & Field Operational Guides**

---

## 🗓️ 8-Week Deliverables Roadmap

| Week | Phase / Focus | Key Deliverable |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Week 01** | **Requirements & Market Framing** | BRD Draft, User Stories, Functional Requirements, Economic Framework |
| **Week 02** | **Data Annotation & ROI Modeling** | Annotation Specs (CV collaboration) & Granular ROI/KPI Model |
| **Week 03** | **RAG Knowledge Base & UI Mockups** | ARC Technical Knowledge Sources & Dashboard Wireframes |
| **Week 04** | **CV Diagnostic Reality Check** | Field Accuracy Validation Report for Disease Diagnostics |
| **Week 05** | **LLM Advisory Evaluation** | LLM Dialect & Agronomic Accuracy Audit Report |
| **Week 06** | **Dashboard & UAT Testing** | Dashboard Specifications & User Acceptance Testing (UAT) Results |
| **Week 07** | **Documentation & Pitch Prep** | End-User Manual & Business Value Pitch Slides |
| **Week 08** | **Final Presentation** | Demo Day Business Value Presentation & Final Summary |

---
